Common Questions

Straight answers, no jargon.

How much does coverage actually cost?

It depends on your age, health, and how much coverage you need — but many people are surprised how affordable it is. I'll always give you real numbers upfront, and I shop across 32+ carriers to find the most affordable fit for your situation.

Do I need a medical exam to qualify?

Not always. Many plans, including final expense and mortgage protection, use simplified underwriting with no exam required. I'll walk you through which options fit your health history.

I've been declined for coverage before — can I still get approved?

Often, yes. Being declined by one carrier doesn't mean you're uninsurable — it usually just means that specific carrier wasn't the right fit. Since I work with 32+ carriers, I can shop your situation around to find one that's more accepting of your health history.

What's the difference between mortgage protection and regular life insurance?

Mortgage protection is essentially life insurance built around your home, with extra built-in benefits like coverage for critical illness or disability. A common misconception is that it's tied to your mortgage — it's not. It's life insurance on you, not the property, so even if you move or pay off your home, your coverage goes with you. Traditional life insurance works the same way but is more general-purpose, built around any need your family has.

What other benefits come with my policy?

Some of the carriers I work with offer added benefits at no extra cost — like a free living will and trust, power of attorney, and even college grant opportunities for your children or grandchildren. On top of that, the payout itself typically goes directly to your beneficiaries tax-free and without going through probate, so your family gets access to it quickly instead of waiting on the courts.

Does it cost more to work with a broker?

No — working with an independent broker doesn't cost you anything extra. Your rate is set by the insurance carrier, not by me, so you pay the same price (often less, since I can shop around for the best fit) as you would going directly to a company. The only difference is you get someone shopping multiple carriers on your behalf, at no added cost.

Is the consultation really free, with no obligation?

Yes. We'll talk through your situation, and I'll explain your options honestly — including telling you if you don't need anything at all. There's never any pressure to buy.

How long does the process take?

Many plans can be approved in as little as a day or two. More complex situations can take longer, but I'll keep you updated every step of the way so you're never left wondering.
